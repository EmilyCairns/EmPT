Privacy

How your information is looked after

EmPT is the training app run by Emily, personal trainer in Perth ([your business name]). This page explains what I collect, why, and who can see it.

What I collect

  • Your details: name, email address and birthday.
  • Your health form: your answers to the standard pre-exercise screening questions, injuries, medical conditions, medications, allergies, pregnancy, goals, lifestyle and an emergency contact.
  • Your training: the workouts I write for you, what you log (weights, reps, how hard a workout felt), your comments and check-ins (for example poor sleep, night shifts or your period), your rep maxes and booked sessions.
  • Messages between you and me in the app's chat.
  • My own notes from our consults.

Why

To plan training that is safe and suits you, to adjust it as you go, to keep in touch, and to know who to contact in an emergency. I don't use your information for anything else, and I never sell it.

Who can see it

  • Only you and me. Each client can only ever see their own information; the database enforces this, not just the app.
  • Team wins is the one exception, and only if you choose to join it: other clients then see your first name and your wins (personal bests, streak seals, finished programs and workout milestones). Never your health form, check-ins, notes or messages. You can stop sharing at any time and your wins disappear from the feed.
  • I may share information with a health professional (for example your GP) or emergency services only if you agree, or if it's needed to keep you safe.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored securely with Supabase, a cloud database service, in its Singapore data centre. Sign-in emails are sent by an email service on my behalf. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which holds the app's code but none of your information.

Keeping it up to date, and deleting it

You can update your health details any time from Update my health details at the bottom of your Home page. Ask me if you'd like a copy of your information or want something corrected or deleted. When you stop training with me, I'll delete your account on request. I may keep your pre-exercise screening record for a period afterwards where industry guidance or my insurer requires it.

Questions or concerns

Message me in the app, or email [your contact email]. If you're not happy with how I've handled your information, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
