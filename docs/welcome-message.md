# Welcome message for clients

Copy this into a text, WhatsApp or email when you add a client's email in the
app. Change anything you like.

---

Hi [name]! 👋

I've set you up on my training app, **EmPT**. It's where you'll find your workouts, log what you lift, see your sessions and chat with me.

**To get started:**
1. Open this link on your phone: https://app.emilycairns.com.au
2. Type your email ([their email]) and tap **Email me a sign-in code**.
3. Type in the 6-digit code from the email. (No password to remember.)
4. Fill in the short health form. It takes about 5 minutes and helps me keep your training safe.

**Save it like an app:**
- **iPhone:** in Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** in Chrome, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

Then open EmPT from your home screen. If it asks you to sign in again, just request a new code.

Any trouble, message me. See you soon!
Emily

---

**Tips**
- Only emails you've added as clients can sign in, so add their email in
  **Edit client** before you send this.
- Codes only last a little while. If one doesn't work, tap **Send a new code**.
